# Specification

![EasyEXG board](/images/hardware/2026/11/easyexg/easyexg_board.jpg)

## Hardware

| | |
|---|---|
| **Revision** | EXG-Front v1 (silkscreen "EasyEXG by PiEEG V1.1") |
| **Channels** | 1 differential input (IN+, IN−) plus REF / driven right leg |
| **Signals** | EMG, ECG, EOG, EEG |
| **Instrumentation amplifier** | TI INA333, gain 9.9×, CMRR ≥ 100 dB |
| **Op-amp** | TI OPA4388 (quad): VCC/2 reference, driven right leg, high-pass + gain, low-pass |
| **Total gain** | 221× (JP1 open) / 1 048× (JP1 bridged) |
| **Bandwidth (−3 dB)** | 0.16–501 Hz |
| **Low-pass** | 500 Hz, 2nd-order Sallen-Key (Q = 0.73) |
| **Output** | 1 analog signal, rests at VCC/2 |
| **Supply** | 3.3 V or 5 V, absolute max 5.5 V |
| **DRL current limit** | 390 kΩ, about 13 µA into the body at 5 V |
| **Electrode connectors** | J1: 3.5 mm TRS jack (CUI SJ-3523-SMT) · J2: 3-pin 2.54 mm header |
| **Output connector** | J3: 4-pin 2.54 mm header (GND, VCC, OUT, VREF) |
| **PCB** | 46.0 × 26.0 × 1.6 mm, 2-layer FR-4, components on top only |
| **Mounting** | 2 × M2 holes (Ø 2.2 mm), 40.2 mm apart |
| **Isolation** | None. Battery operation is required |
| **Licence** | CC BY-NC 4.0 |

## Gain and input range

| JP1 | Use | Total gain | Band (−3 dB) | Max input before clipping (5 V / 3.3 V) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open | EMG, EOG | **221×** | 0.16–501 Hz | 10.9 mV / 7.2 mV |
| Bridged | ECG, EEG | **1 048×** | 0.16–501 Hz | 2.3 mV / 1.5 mV |

## Signal chain

| Stage | Part | Function | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | INA333 (U1) | Instrumentation amplifier, G = 1 + 100 kΩ / 11.2 kΩ | 9.9× |
| 2 | OPA4388 (U2C) | 0.16 Hz high-pass + non-inverting gain (JP1 selects the gain) | 22× / 106× |
| 3 | OPA4388 (U2D) | 500 Hz 2nd-order Sallen-Key low-pass (Q = 0.73) | 1× |
| — | OPA4388 (U2A) | Mid-supply reference VREF = VCC/2 | — |
| — | OPA4388 (U2B) | Driven right leg (DRL), 390 kΩ current limit | — |

## Frequency response

![Simulated gain versus frequency](/images/hardware/2026/11/easyexg/easyexg_amp_response.png)

Simulated in ngspice with a 10 MHz OPA4388 model:

- ✅ Instrumentation amplifier gain is 9.9×, as designed.
- ✅ The 0.16 Hz high-pass keeps the true ECG shape.
- ✅ The 500 Hz 2nd-order low-pass covers the whole EMG band (20–450 Hz) and removes noise above it.
- ✅ The INA333 rejects mains hum with CMRR ≥ 100 dB.
- ✅ The DRL loop is stable, with at most 2 dB of peaking (around 1.3–1.5 kHz).

### Design note: R8 = 10 MΩ

R8 (DRL feedback resistor) is **10 MΩ** (Yageo RC0603FR-0710ML). With 1 MΩ the right-leg drive is too weak: simulated 50 Hz common mode at the input is −46.8 dB. With 10 MΩ it improves to **−55.3 dB**, with peaking still below +2 dB. R9 stays at 390 kΩ, which limits the current into the body to about 13 µA at 5 V.

<Callout type="info">
  Simulations use idealised electrode (51 kΩ ∥ 47 nF) and body (200 pF mains coupling) models, so real hum levels will differ.
</Callout>

## Operating notes

- OUT rests at **VCC/2** with no signal.
- Sample EMG at ≥ 1 kHz, ECG / EOG at ≥ 250 Hz, EEG at 250 Hz.
- Power the board from the same voltage as your ADC range.
- Power from a battery or an isolated supply while electrodes are on a person.
