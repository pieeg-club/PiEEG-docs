# Partnership

If you are thinking about EEG or BCI and wondering whether you should build your own hardware — it is slow, expensive, and distracts from the thing you actually care about. PiEEG exists so researchers, startups, and institutions can skip years of hardware pain and focus on applications, software, and results.

## Who We Partner With

### Startups & Companies

For teams that want EEG in their product without spending years developing hardware. We offer:

- **White-label hardware** — PiEEG devices ready to ship
- **Custom firmware** — tailored to your application
- **Software integration** — PiEEG Server and SDK support
- **Technical consulting** — signal processing and machine learning guidance

If your roadmap includes EEG and your timeline is shorter than "sometime after your funding runs out," it is worth talking.

### Universities & Research Labs

We work with universities and research groups to:

- Provide PiEEG devices for research
- Support teaching labs and student projects
- Enable more experiments, not fewer approvals

If your research budget is tight but your ambitions are not, PiEEG is built for you.

### Hackathons & Research Programs

We support hackathons, student initiatives, and applied neuroscience programs with hardware, software, and technical guidance.

## What You Get

- Proven, research-grade hardware spanning 8 to 32 channels
- MIT-licensed software and SDKs with no vendor lock-in
- Direct access to the engineering team that designs and builds the platform
- A global open-source community and documentation
