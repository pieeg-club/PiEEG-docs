# Careers

PiEEG is more than hardware — it is a movement to democratize neuroscience. The team builds tools that empower researchers, students, and innovators worldwide to explore the brain without prohibitive costs.

## Why Join PiEEG?

- **Global impact** — Used by universities, researchers, and makers worldwide.
- **Open-source culture** — MIT-licensed software with transparent development.
- **Remote friendly** — Work from anywhere with flexible hours and async collaboration.

## Areas of Interest

### Hardware Engineering

PCB design, analog circuit development, signal processing, and embedded systems — including EEG/EMG/ECG amplifier design.

### Software Engineering

Real-time systems, streaming servers, SDKs, web dashboards, edge AI, and XR integrations. The core software stack is TypeScript and Python.

### Research & Machine Learning

EEG feature extraction, neural decoding, brain-computer interface algorithms, and applied AI for biosignals.

### Community, Docs & Education

Technical writing, tutorials, translations, developer relations, and community support.
