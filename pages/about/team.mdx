# Team

PiEEG is built by engineers, scientists, and hackers working on the future of accessible neuroscience. The team combines expertise in hardware design, signal processing, machine learning, and real-time software systems.

## Leadership

### Ildar Rakhmatulin — Founder & Lead Engineer

PhD scientist, engineer, and entrepreneur in bioscience. With an academic background spanning Imperial College London, Heriot-Watt University, and the University of Edinburgh, Ildar has published numerous papers on deep neural networks for EEG feature extraction, low-cost brain-computer interfaces, and applied AI. His work is widely cited in neurotechnology and BCI research. He launched PiEEG on Crowd Supply and raised five times the initial goal.

- **Role:** Founder & Lead Engineer
- **Location:** Edinburgh, Scotland, UK
- **Focus areas:** PhD research, hardware design, signal processing, machine learning, Udemy instruction
