# Community & Open Source

Every line of PiEEG software, and every software decision, is made in the open. The community brings together researchers, makers, and neuroscience enthusiasts building the future of brain-computer interfaces.

<Callout type="info">
  **Licensing:** PiEEG **software and SDKs are MIT licensed** (including PiEEG Server). Hardware designs are proprietary. You can fork, modify, and build freely on the software side.
</Callout>

## Ways to Contribute

- **Write code** — Contribute to firmware, the server, SDKs, or the web UI. All skill levels are welcome.
- **Improve docs** — Write tutorials, fix typos, translate pages, or create examples.
- **Help others** — Answer questions on Discord, review pull requests, or mentor new users.
- **Build hardware** — Design new shields, cases, or electrodes and share your mods.

## Community Principles

- **Truly open** — MIT-licensed software and SDKs (PiEEG Server included). Fork and build freely on the software side.
- **Community first** — Decisions are made in the open with a transparent roadmap.
- **Built to last** — Long-term support, no planned obsolescence, backward compatibility.
- **Science for all** — Democratizing neuroscience tools with accessible pricing and an educational focus.
