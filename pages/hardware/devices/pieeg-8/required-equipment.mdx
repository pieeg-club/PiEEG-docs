# What's in the Box

## Included

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **1 × PiEEG-8 shield** | 8-channel ADS1299 board with 40-pin Raspberry Pi header and 2.54 mm electrode header |
| **Quick-start manual** | Printed installation guide for PiEEG devices |

![PiEEG-8 package: box, printed manual and the shield](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieeg8_box.jpg)

## Not included

| Item | Requirements | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Raspberry Pi** | Raspberry Pi 4 or Raspberry Pi 5, with a microSD card (16 GB or more) and Raspberry Pi OS | [How to Start](/hardware/devices/pieeg-8/setup) |
| **5 V power bank** | 5 V output, 3–3.5 A, capacity up to 10,000 mAh. Powers the Pi, the shield and the monitor. | [Power bank](/hardware/accessories/power-bank) |
| **Electrodes** | 8 EEG electrodes (dry or wet Ag/AgCl) with 2.54 mm connectors, plus **2 ear-clip electrodes** for reference and bias | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |
| **EEG cap** | Holds the electrodes in place on the scalp (10-20 system). Ready-made nylon cap or DIY. | [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap) |
| **Monitor** | Portable monitor powered from the power bank or the Pi, never from a wall socket | [Safety](/hardware/devices/pieeg-8/safety) |
| **Keyboard and mouse** | Wired USB. Wireless devices add radio noise. | [Noise elimination](/hardware/signal-quality/noise-elimination) |
| **Conductive gel** | Only for wet electrodes | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |

<Callout type="info">
  **Headless option:** you can skip the monitor, keyboard and mouse and open the PiEEG Server dashboard from a laptop on the same network. The laptop must run **on battery** and must never be cabled to the Pi or the electrodes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Battery only.** Every device that is cabled to the PiEEG-8 (the Pi, the monitor, the keyboard) must run from the power bank. Never use a wall charger, a desk power supply or a USB port of a mains-powered computer.
</Callout>

## Checklist

- [ ] PiEEG-8 shield
- [ ] Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 with Raspberry Pi OS on a microSD card
- [ ] Charged 5 V / 3 A power bank and a short, thick USB cable
- [ ] 8 electrodes and 2 ear clips
- [ ] EEG cap (and gel if you use wet electrodes)
- [ ] Battery-powered monitor, wired keyboard and mouse (or a battery-powered laptop for headless use)
