# Electrodes & Battery

## Electrode connections

IronBCI-8 has three kinds of electrode inputs.

| Input | Count | Electrode | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Channels 1–8** | 8 | Dry or wet Ag/AgCl, in the cap | Each channel measures the voltage between its electrode and the reference |
| **Reference** | 1 | Ear clip on one earlobe | The common 0 µV point that all 8 channels are measured against |
| **Bias** | 1 | Ear clip on the other earlobe | Actively drives the body to cancel common-mode noise such as 50/60 Hz mains hum |

![IronBCI-8 electrode, reference and bias connections](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_connection.png)

The electrode header is a standard **2.54 mm** pin header, so most EEG cables with 2.54 mm (0.1") female connectors fit. Check the pitch before using a third-party cap. See [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap).

<Callout type="info">
  The **reference and bias clips matter most**. A loose ear clip makes every channel noisy at once. Clean the earlobes and make sure both clips sit firmly before you look at anything else.
</Callout>

## Suggested montage

These are the 8 positions used for the dry-electrode tests in the IronBCI paper. They spread across the front, centre, sides and back of the head.

| Position | Area | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **F7**, **F8** | Front left and right, side | Eye movement, frontal activity |
| **Fz** | Front centre | Blinks, attention |
| **C3**, **C4** | Centre left and right | Motor activity (hands) |
| **T5**, **T6** | Back left and right, side | Temporal activity |
| **Pz** | Back centre, top | Alpha rhythm |

![International 10-20 electrode positions](/images/hardware/2024/08/New-Bitmap-Image-264x300.jpg)

Assign them to channels 1–8 in any order, and keep a note of which channel went where, because the software only knows channel numbers.

## Fitting the cap

1. Measure the head and pick the right cap size, or adjust the 3D-printed headset.
2. Find **Cz** (halfway between nose and the back of the head, halfway between the ears) and align the cap with it.
3. **Dry electrodes:** part the hair under each electrode so it touches the scalp.
4. **Wet electrodes:** inject a small amount of conductive gel under each electrode and work it through the hair with the blunt syringe tip.
5. Attach the ear clips to clean earlobes.
6. Keep the IronBCI-8 close to the head, with short electrode leads.

## Battery

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Single-cell LiPo |
| **Capacity** | 200 mAh recommended |
| **Connector** | 2.0 mm JST, on the underside of the board stack |
| **Charging** | Micro USB, 5 V, on the power board |

![Battery connector on the underside of IronBCI-8](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_battery.jpg)

<Callout type="error">
  **Check the polarity every time you use a new battery.** JST batteries are not all wired the same way. Match the black wire to **GND** on the board. Reversed polarity **will damage the device**.
</Callout>

![GND marking for the battery connector](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_polarity.jpg)

## Switches

| Mode | Switch 1 (battery) | Switch 2 (data) |
|---|---|---|
| **Recording** | ON | ON |
| **Charging** | ON | OFF |
| **Off / storage** | OFF | OFF |

![Switch 1 turns on the battery, Switch 2 starts the data flow](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_switches.jpg)

## LEDs

| LED | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LED 1** (power board) | Charging |
| **LED 2** (power board) | Battery fully charged |
| **BLE LED** | On when a Bluetooth connection is active |
