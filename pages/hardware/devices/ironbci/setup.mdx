# How to Start

From the box to live EEG over Bluetooth, in seven steps.

<Steps>

### Gather the parts

You need the IronBCI-8, a 200 mAh LiPo battery with a 2.0 mm JST connector, 8 electrodes with two ear clips, a cap, and a Bluetooth device to receive the data. See [What's in the Box](/hardware/devices/ironbci/required-equipment).

### Connect the battery

The battery connector is on the **underside** of the board stack. Check the polarity, then plug the battery in.

![Battery connected to the bottom of IronBCI-8](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_battery.jpg)

<Callout type="error">
  Some JST batteries have reversed polarity. Match the battery's black wire to **GND** on the board. A reversed battery **will damage the device**.
</Callout>

![GND marking on the battery connector](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_polarity.jpg)

### Charge the battery

1. Set **Switch 1 ON** and **Switch 2 OFF**.
2. Plug a Micro USB cable (5 V) into the charging port.
3. **LED 1** lights while charging. **LED 2** lights when the battery is full.
4. Unplug the USB cable before you go on.

![Charging LEDs: LED 1 charging, LED 2 charged](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_charge_leds.jpg)

<Callout type="warning">
  Never wear IronBCI-8 or record from a person while it is charging.
</Callout>

### Connect the electrodes

| Connector | Electrode | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Channels 1–8** | 8 EEG electrodes in the cap | Scalp positions of the 10-20 system |
| **Reference** | Ear-clip electrode | One earlobe |
| **Bias** | Ear-clip electrode | The other earlobe |

![Electrode, reference and bias connections](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_connection.png)

Full details and a suggested montage: [Electrodes & Battery](/hardware/devices/ironbci/connections).

### Switch on

Unplug the USB cable and set **both switches ON**. IronBCI-8 starts advertising over Bluetooth Low Energy and is ready to connect.

![Switch 1 turns on the battery, Switch 2 starts the data flow](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_switches.jpg)

| Mode | Switch 1 | Switch 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Recording** | ON | ON |
| **Charging** | ON | OFF |

### Connect over Bluetooth

You don't need to pair it in your system's Bluetooth settings. The software finds and connects to it directly. It usually appears as **`EAREEG`** (some units show **`IronBCI`**).

<Tabs items={['Browser (fastest)', 'Android app', 'PiEEG Server', 'Python']}>
<Tabs.Tab>

1. Open **[cloud.pieeg.com](https://cloud.pieeg.com)** in **Chrome or Edge**.
2. In the Session Lobby, open the **PiEEG Devices** tab and choose **IronBCI**.
3. Pick the device in the browser's Bluetooth window.

No install, and the data stays in your browser.

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

1. Install [`mobile_sdk.apk`](https://github.com/pieeg-club/ironbci/tree/master/mobile_app) on an Android phone.
2. In the app's settings, choose **8 ch**.
3. Connect, and watch the signal live. Press **Save** to record, **Stop** to finish, then share the file.

</Tabs.Tab>

When the connection is made, the **BLE LED** turns on and 8 channels stream at 250 samples per second.

![BLE LED on when connected](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_ble_led.jpg)

### Check the signal

Sit still, relax the face and run these quick tests. If they behave as described, you are recording real biosignals.

| Test | What you should see |
|---|---|
| **Sit still for 30 seconds** | All 8 channels settle. Flat lines or huge values mean a loose electrode. |
| **Close your eyes for 10 seconds** | Alpha (8–13 Hz) rises on the back-of-head channels, then drops when the eyes open |
| **Blink a few times** | Large slow spikes on the front channels |
| **Clench the jaw** | Bursts of high-frequency noise on all channels. That is muscle (EMG), not brain activity. |

![Alpha rhythm with eyes closed, recorded with IronBCI-8](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci8_alpha.png)
