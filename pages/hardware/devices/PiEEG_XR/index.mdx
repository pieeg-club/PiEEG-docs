# PiEEG XR

**Bring your brain and face signals into virtual reality.**

PiEEG XR is a brain-computer interface for VR headsets. A **silicone face mask** with **10 dry Ag/AgCl electrodes** replaces the headset's facial interface, and a small **BCI box** on the head strap streams the signals over **Bluetooth LE**. Attention, relaxation, blinks and facial expressions reach VR apps, social VR (VRChat via OSC) and the browser, with no proprietary SDK.

![PiEEG XR on a Meta Quest 3: face mask and BCI box](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_render.jpg)

<Callout type="warning">
  PiEEG XR is a **research device, not a medical device**. Power it from a **5 V power bank or the headset's battery**, never from a wall charger. Read [Safety](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/safety) before the first use.
</Callout>

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Channels** | 8 (PiEEG XR) or 16 (PiEEG XR-16), plus reference and bias |
| **Electrodes** | 10 flat dry Ag/AgCl electrodes in a silicone mask (8 channels, reference, bias) |
| **Converters** | TI ADS1299, 24-bit (two chips on 16-channel versions) |
| **Sample rate** | 250 SPS |
| **Noise** | 1.0 µV peak-to-peak |
| **Microcontroller** | STM32WB (Arm Cortex-M4 + M0+ radio core) |
| **Wireless** | Bluetooth LE 5, appears as `PiEEG XR` |
| **Power** | 5 V over USB-C, from a power bank or the headset's battery strap |
| **Headsets** | Meta Quest 3, Meta Quest, PICO and others. Each unit is made for one headset model. |
| **Software** | BodyPress (Quest and Android), PiEEG Cloud, PiEEG Server, VRChat OSC |

## How it works

```
 Face mask ──cable──► BCI box ──BLE──► Quest (BodyPress app)
 10 dry electrodes    ADS1299 +        ├ cloud.pieeg.com
 (8 ch, ref, bias)    STM32WB          ├ PiEEG Server ─OSC─► VRChat
                         ▲             └ Android (BodyPress)
     5 V power bank ─────┘
```

1. **Touch:** the mask's electrodes rest on the forehead and temples, under the headset.
2. **Measure:** the ADS1299 in the BCI box digitises all channels, 250 times per second.
3. **Send:** the STM32WB streams the data over Bluetooth LE to the headset, a phone, a browser or a PC.
4. **Use:** apps turn it into attention and relaxation levels, blink and expression events, or avatar parameters.

## What you can measure

| Signal | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **EEG** | Brain rhythms from the forehead and temples: attention, relaxation, focus |
| **EOG** | Blinks and eye movements, very clear on the front electrodes |
| **EMG** | Facial expressions: jaw clench, smile, frown (used by the Face Trainer game) |
