# Safety

**Read this before putting the mask on anyone.**

<Callout type="warning">
  PiEEG XR is a **research device, not a medical device**, and has not been certified by any regulator for use with the human body. You use it at your own risk.
</Callout>

## Rules

1. **Battery only.** Power the BCI box from a power bank or a battery head strap. Never from a wall charger, and never through a cable to a computer that is on mains power.
2. **No diagnosis.** Attention, relaxation and expression values are research indicators. Don't use them to diagnose, monitor or treat any condition, or as a measure of mood or ability.
3. **Implanted devices.** Don't use it on a person with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device without a physician's approval.
4. **Skin.** Use clean, intact skin. Stop if the skin becomes irritated, and clean off any gel after use.
5. **VR safety.** Follow your headset maker's safety guidance. Clear the play area, and take breaks.
6. **Flashing.** Update firmware with the mask off the face.

## Power while worn

| Power source | While worn | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Power bank or battery head strap** | ✅ Safe | No path to the mains. |
| **Headset's own battery** (headset not charging) | ✅ Safe | Still fully on battery. |
| **Wall charger** | ❌ Never | Connects the person to the mains through the electrodes. |
| **USB cable to a PC on mains power** | ❌ Never | Same reason. |
| **Headset plugged in to charge** | ❌ Not while worn with PiEEG XR | The charger links the mains to the headset and the mask. |

## Hygiene

- Wipe the electrodes and the silicone with an alcohol pad before and after each user.
- Let them dry fully before the next use.
