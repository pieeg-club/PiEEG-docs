# Frequently Asked Questions

### The device is not visible

This is usually a power supply problem.

- Use a **USB-to-USB** cable (USB on one end, USB on the other). This is the most reliable option.
- To connect to a battery, you can use a **USB to USB-C adapter** on the other end.
- Open the case from the box and check whether an **LED lights up inside** when you connect power. If no LED comes on, check your cable.

See [Required Equipment](https://docs.pieeg.com/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/required-equipment/) for the recommended power setup.

### The silicone mask fell off

Use your headset's **original mask together with our silicone insert**, and adjust it manually until it sits firmly in place with no gaps or bending.

More detail on [How to Connect PiEEG XR](https://docs.pieeg.com/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/connections/).

### I can't connect to the data

This is usually a **BLE** issue — the fix is to update the microcontroller firmware.

See [Service → Update Framework](https://docs.pieeg.com/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/Service/) for step-by-step instructions.

### The data is too noisy

Poor contact or environmental noise is the most common cause.

See [Poor Signal / Noise](#) and the [Noise Elimination Guide](https://docs.pieeg.com/hardware/signal-quality/noise-elimination/).
