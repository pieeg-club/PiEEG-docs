# Required Equipment

To start measuring Bio Data with PiEEG XR, it is necessary to have the following parts:

- Power Bank: A 5V power bank 
- USB-C cable to connect PiEEG XR to the power bank. 
Important: Use only USB-C power supplies designed for power supply

- Headset. We recommend using a headset with a battery inside to have the opportunity to supply power for PiEEG XR
