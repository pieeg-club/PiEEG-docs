# What's in the Box

## Included

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Silicone face mask** | Replaces the headset's facial interface, with 10 flat dry Ag/AgCl electrodes and a cable to the BCI box |
| **BCI box** | Round enclosure with the ADS1299 board and STM32WB Bluetooth controller, held by the headset's head strap |

![PiEEG XR face mask and BCI box](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_back.jpg)

## Headset compatibility

PiEEG XR works with **Meta Quest 3**, **Meta Quest**, **PICO** and other headset models.

<Callout type="warning">
  The mask and the box are **made for one specific headset model**. For example, a unit built for the Meta Quest 3 fits **only** the Meta Quest 3. Check the model when you order. A DIY kit for other headsets and masks is coming soon.
</Callout>

## Not included

| Item | Requirements | More |
|---|---|---|
| **VR headset** | The model your unit was made for | [Mask & Electrodes](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/connections) |
| **5 V power source** | A **power bank**, or a **headset strap with a built-in battery** that has a USB output | [Safety](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/safety) |
| **USB cable** | **USB-A to USB-C** works most reliably. USB-C to USB-C may not power the box from some supplies. | See below |
| **A device to receive data** | The headset itself (BodyPress app), an Android phone, or a computer with Chrome / Edge or Python | [Software](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/software) |
| **Alcohol pads** | For cleaning the skin and the electrodes before each session | [Recording During Activity](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/Application) |
| **Conductive gel (optional)** | A little gel or saline on the dry electrodes makes the signal steadier during movement | [Recording During Activity](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/Application) |

## Power

The BCI box takes **5 V over USB-C**.

![USB-A to USB-C cable](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_cable.jpg)

<Callout type="info">
  **Use a USB-A to USB-C cable.** Some USB-C power supplies only switch on for devices that negotiate USB-C power, and the box may get no power from a USB-C to USB-C cable. If the device isn't visible, try a USB-A to USB-C cable first.
</Callout>

**Best option: a battery head strap.** A headset strap with a built-in battery and a USB port powers both the headset and the PiEEG XR, with no extra power bank to carry.

![Headset strap with a built-in battery](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_headset.jpg)

## Checklist

- [ ] PiEEG XR mask and BCI box, made for your headset model
- [ ] Charged power bank or battery head strap
- [ ] USB-A to USB-C cable
- [ ] Alcohol pads (and gel, optional)
- [ ] BodyPress on the headset or phone, or Chrome / Edge on a computer
