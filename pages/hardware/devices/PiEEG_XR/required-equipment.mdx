# Required Equipment

To start measuring bio-data with the PiEEG XR, you will need the following parts in addition to the device itself:

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Power bank (5 V) | Supplies power to the PiEEG XR |
| USB-C cable | Connects the PiEEG XR to the power bank |
| VR headset | Runs the XR experience |

## Power

The PiEEG XR is powered by a **5 V power bank** connected over a **USB-C cable**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Use only USB-C supplies rated for power delivery. For the most reliable connection, we recommend a **USB-C to USB-A cable**.

![PiEEG XR USB-C connection](/images/hardware/2026/01/PiEEG_XR_USB_C.bmp)

## Headset

We recommend a **headset with a built-in battery**, so it can also supply power to the PiEEG XR.

![PiEEG XR headset](/images/hardware/2026/01/PiEEG_XR_Headset.bmp)


## Compatibility

The PiEEG XR works with **Meta Quest**, **Meta Quest 3**, **PICO**, and other headset models.  

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> The device housing and mask are configured for the specific headset model they were made for. For example, a unit built for the **Meta Quest 3** fits **only** the Meta Quest 3.   

If you want to adapt the setup for a different headset or mask, a **DIY kit** manual is coming soon. It will let you customize the configuration for any device and mask.
