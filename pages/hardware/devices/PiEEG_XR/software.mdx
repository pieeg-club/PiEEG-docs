# Device test

This page is a short introduction to getting PiEEG XR running with the software. For full details, follow the linked videos below.

> [!NOTE]
> You don't need a Meta Quest to try PiEEG XR — you can test it directly through the **PiEEG Server** in your browser.

## 1. Connect via the Live Demo

1. Open [pieeg.com](https://pieeg.com) and click **Try Live Demo**.
2. Make sure **Bluetooth is turned on** on your device.
3. Connect to the **`PiEEG XR`** device over **BLE**.

Once connected, live data begins streaming.

## 2. Test the Signal

1. Put the mask on your face.
2. Watch the incoming data and run a few simple tests — **blinking** and **chewing** produce clear, visible signals.
3. Experiment with the **band-pass filter** and other settings.
4. **Save your data**, or test facial mimics through the built-in **mini-games**.

> [!TIP]
> To test mimics, open **Mini-Games** and choose **Face Trainer**.
