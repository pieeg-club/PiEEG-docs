# Mask & Electrodes

PiEEG XR has two parts that attach to your headset:

1. A **silicone face mask** with built-in electrodes. It replaces (or sits inside) the headset's facial interface and touches your forehead and temples.
2. A **BCI box**, connected to the mask by a cable and held by the head strap. It holds the amplifier and the Bluetooth controller.

![PiEEG XR: mask and BCI box](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_back.jpg)

## Electrodes

The mask has **10 flat dry silver / silver-chloride (Ag/AgCl) electrodes**. They pick up biosignals without gel, and also work with a little gel when you need a steadier signal.

![Flat dry Ag/AgCl electrodes](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_electrodes.jpg)

| Electrode | Count | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Channels 1–8** | 8 | Signal electrodes on the forehead and temples |
| **Reference** | 1 | The common 0 µV point for all channels, centre-left of the forehead |
| **Bias** | 1 | Cancels common-mode noise such as mains hum, centre-right of the forehead |

## Electrode layout

The electrodes sit in 10 holes (1 mm) around the top of the mask:

![PiEEG XR electrode positions on the mask](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_electrode_map.png)

| Position on the mask (as drawn) | Electrodes | Picks up |
|---|---|---|
| **Left side**, lower and upper | Ch1, Ch2 | Temple: eye movement, jaw and temple muscles |
| **Left forehead** | Ch3, Ch4 | Frontal EEG, blinks |
| **Centre forehead** | Ref, Bias | Reference and bias |
| **Right forehead** | Ch5, Ch6 | Frontal EEG, blinks |
| **Right side**, upper and lower | Ch7, Ch8 | Temple: eye movement, jaw and temple muscles |

Because all electrodes are on the front of the head, PiEEG XR is strongest at **frontal EEG, blinks, eye movements and facial expressions**. The classic eyes-closed alpha test needs electrodes at the back of the head, which only the academic version has.

## Attaching to the headset

**The mask** clips into the headset with four buckles, two at the back and two at the front. See [How to Start](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/setup#replace-the-face-mask).

**Or keep your own mask:** take out only the **silicone insert** and fit it into the headset's original facial interface.

**The BCI box** sits against the back of the headset, held in place by the head strap.

![Mounting the BCI box](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_mount_steps.jpg)

## Getting good contact

<Callout type="info">
  A stable, well-positioned mask is the **single biggest factor** in clean signal quality.
</Callout>

- **Clean the skin** with an alcohol pad and let it dry. Oil, sweat and make-up raise the contact resistance.
- **Tighten the strap** so the mask presses evenly. No gaps, no bending.
- **Check the silicone insert** is seated all the way round. If the soft part has shifted or fallen out, push it back into place.
- **Add a little gel or saline** to the electrodes if the signal is unsteady, especially when you move.
- **Clean the electrodes** with an alcohol pad after each use.
