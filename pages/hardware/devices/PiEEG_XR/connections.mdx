---
title: "How to connect PiEEG XR"
---

The BCI unit is located inside the enclosure. The enclosure features mounting wings on the bottom that attach directly to the side straps of the Meta Quest.
General view of device. Example for Meta Quest S3
![Image](/images/hardware/2026/01/pieeg_XR_back.png)  

To establish the setup, you need to remove the old mask from the Meta Quest and put the PiEEG XR 
as shown in this image 
![Image](/images/hardware/2026/01/pieeg_xr1.jpg)    

After that, need put box with the brain-computer interface inside the headset 
According to this image 
![Image](/images/hardware/2026/01/pieeg_xr1.jpg)    

Step 1 — Mount the PiEEG Enclosure
Detach the side strap from your Meta Quest 3S.

Insert the strap through the mounting wings on the bottom of the PiEEG enclosure.
Reattach the side strap back onto the headset.

Step 2 — Replace the Facial Interface
Remove the original facial interface (mask) from the Meta Quest 3S and snap the new interface into place.

Step 3 — Power On
Connect your external power bank to the PiEEG XR to power on the device.
