# How to Start

From the box to live signals in your headset or browser, in seven steps. The example shows a **Meta Quest 3**.

<Steps>

### Gather the parts

You need the PiEEG XR made for your headset, a power bank or battery head strap, a USB-A to USB-C cable, and alcohol pads. See [What's in the Box](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/required-equipment).

### Replace the face mask

1. Remove the headset's original facial interface.
2. Line up the four buckles of the PiEEG XR mask with the headset.
3. Press to snap in the **two back** buckles.
4. Press to snap in the **two front** buckles.

![Replacing the face mask in four steps](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_mask_steps.jpg)

<Callout type="info">
  **Prefer your own mask?** Remove just the **silicone insert** from the PiEEG XR mask and fit it into your headset's original facial interface. Adjust it until it sits firmly with no gaps or bending.
</Callout>

### Mount the BCI box

1. Remove the head strap from the headset.
2. Place the BCI box against the headset, then reattach the strap so it holds the box.
3. Route the cable from the mask to the box.

![Mounting the BCI box under the head strap](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_mount_steps.jpg)

### Power on

Connect the **5 V power bank** (or battery strap) to the box with the USB-A to USB-C cable. The box starts advertising over Bluetooth as **`PiEEG XR`**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Battery power only. Never connect the box to a wall charger or to a computer on mains power while you're wearing it.
</Callout>

### Clean and put it on

1. Wipe the forehead and temples with an alcohol pad, and let the skin dry.
2. Wipe the electrodes in the mask.
3. Put the headset on and tighten the strap so the mask presses **firmly and evenly** on the face.

### Connect

<Tabs items={['On the headset', 'In the browser', 'Android']}>
<Tabs.Tab>

1. Install **[BodyPress](https://www.meta.com/en-gb/experiences/bodypress/1271355469389420/)** from the Meta Quest Store.
2. Open it and connect to **`PiEEG XR`**.

[How to launch it (short video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/Bs9vT9CmJcE)

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

No headset needed for a first test.

1. Open **[cloud.pieeg.com](https://cloud.pieeg.com)** in **Chrome or Edge**, with Bluetooth on.
2. In the Session Lobby, open the **PiEEG Devices** tab and choose **PiEEG XR** (or **PiEEG XR-16**).
3. Pick `PiEEG XR` in the browser's Bluetooth window.

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

1. Install **[BodyPress](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bodypress.governorhq)** from Google Play.
2. Open it and connect to **`PiEEG XR`**.

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

### Check the signal

Sit still, relax the face and wait a few seconds for the filter to settle. Then:

| Test | What you should see |
|---|---|
| **Every channel moving** | A gentle line on each channel. A perfectly flat line means that electrode isn't touching. |
| **Blink hard 3–5 times** | Large, sharp spikes, strongest on the front (forehead) channels |
| **Look left, then right** | Slower swings, in opposite directions on the left and right channels |
| **Clench the jaw** | A burst of high-frequency fuzz that stops when you relax |

![Blink test: groups of 4, 3, 2 and 1 blinks on all 8 channels](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_blink.png)

<Callout type="info">
  **The blink test is your one-second sanity check.** If blinking shows nothing on the front channels, those electrodes aren't touching. Reseat the mask and tighten the strap before going further.
</Callout>

Want to try facial expressions? In PiEEG Cloud, open **Mini-Games → Face Trainer**. [Watch the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g-XgVWwQzwE).

</Steps>
