# Signals & Projects

What real PiEEG XR recordings look like, open data to practise with, and projects to build on.

## Example signal: blinking 4-3-2-1

Blinking 4 times, then 3, 2 and 1, with the mask's dry electrodes. The signal is band-pass filtered to 1–40 Hz and each channel is shifted down for clarity. Every group of blinks is clearly visible on all 8 channels.

![PiEEG XR: 8 channels, blinking 4-3-2-1 times, band-pass 1–40 Hz](/images/hardware/2026/10/pieegxr_blink.png)

<Callout type="info">
  Blinks, eye movements and facial expressions are large on the forehead electrodes. That makes PiEEG XR great for expression and eye-based control, and means you should mark or remove them before analysing brain rhythms.
</Callout>

## Dataset

In the [`Datasets`](https://github.com/pieeg-club/PiEEG_XR_Hardware/tree/main/Datasets) folder of the PiEEG XR repository. 8 channels, about 250 samples per second, values in µV.

| File | Content | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `Raw Data EEG Blinking 4-3-2-1.csv` | Raw data, with the usual DC offset | 3,805 samples (about 15 s) |
| `Data Band Pass (1-40 Hz) EEG Blinking 4-3-2-1.csv` | The same test after a 1–40 Hz band-pass filter | 3,015 samples (about 12 s) |

**Columns:** `timestamp` (Unix time, seconds), `ch1`…`ch8`.

## Projects

### Brain-reactive VRChat avatar

Relax to raise your alpha power and watch your avatar's ears perk up. PiEEG Server sends five EEG band values to VRChat over OSC, and the avatar's animator or PhysBones react to them. See [Software → VRChat avatar](/hardware/devices/PiEEG_XR/software#brain-reactive-vrchat-avatar), the [tutorial](https://www.pieeg.com/tutorials/brain-reactive-vrchat-avatar) and the [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uKa9QgcLvSA).

### Face Trainer

A mini-game in PiEEG Cloud that uses the mask's muscle signals to recognise facial expressions. [Watch the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g-XgVWwQzwE).

### VR games with BodyPress

See your signals as an overlay in VR, or play: [VR overlay demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KBcBU8NnY2E) · [tennis with PiEEG XR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vkuwRFJJNxQ).

### WebXR

PiEEG XR was built for browser-based XR: attention, relaxation and focus drive avatar behaviour in a WebXR scene, with no native app. See the [PiEEG XR paper](https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202606.0016.v1).
