### Step 1 — Upload Firmware
Connect the ESP-32 S3 via USB-C to your computer and upload firmware via the next link        
https://firmware.pieeg.com/  
![Image](/images/hardware/2026/01/octopos_1.jpg)

###  Step 2: PiEEG-Server 
Octopus 16  integrated to pieeg -server.  
Open the PiEEG website pieeg.com and run the PiEEG server with  Octopus 16

![Image](/images/hardware/2026/01/octopos_2.jpg)
