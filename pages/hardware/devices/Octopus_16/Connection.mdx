# How to start. Connections with Octopus 16

This page explains how to physically connect your Octopus 16 board, Seeed Studio XIAO microcontroller, and electrodes before starting a signal acquisition session.

Pay attention to the right connection
![Image](/images/hardware/2026/01/octopos_5.jpg)


### Step 1 — Mount the Seeed Studio XIAO Board
Align your Seeed Studio XIAO microcontroller board (such as the ESP32-C3 or ESP32-S6) with the socket headers on the Octopus 16 mainboard[cite: 1]. Ensure the pin alignment matches correctly and press down gently until fully seated before applying power.
See the right position in this image

### Step 2 — Connect the BLE antenna 
BLE antenna  usually comes with the ESP32 S3

### Step 3 — Power On
 **Powering the Device:** Connect a clean battery supply or power source to the board. Battery power is strongly recommended to isolate the device from mains noise and ensure safe, high-quality biosignal capture.
