# Safety

**Read this before anyone puts it on.**

<Callout type="warning">
  Octopus 16 is **not a medical device** and has not been certified by any regulator for use with the human body. It is a learning and experimentation tool. You use it at your own risk.
</Callout>

## Rules

1. **Power bank only while worn.** Wear Octopus 16 on battery power only, never while it's plugged into a computer or a wall charger. The board has no isolation from its power supply.
2. **Flash off the head.** USB from a computer is only for flashing, with the board off the head.
3. **No diagnosis.** Band power, focus and relax are uncalibrated learning indicators. Never use them for diagnosis, or as a measure of mood or ability.
4. **Implanted devices.** Don't use it on a person with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device without a physician's approval.
5. **Skin.** Use clean, intact skin. Stop if the skin becomes irritated or the pins press too hard.
6. **Pin care.** Press the pins straight down. Clean them with isopropyl alcohol, never oil or grease.

## Power while worn

| Power source | While worn | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Small 5 V power bank** | ✅ Safe | The board is on its own battery, with no path to the mains. |
| **Laptop USB** | ❌ Not while worn | USB from a computer is only for flashing. A plugged-in laptop links the electrodes to the mains. |
| **Wall charger** | ❌ Never | Connects the person to the mains through the electrodes. |

## In the classroom

- An adult checks the power source before each session.
- Flash and charge boards **before** the lesson, with the boards off the head.
- Clean the pins with isopropyl alcohol between users.
