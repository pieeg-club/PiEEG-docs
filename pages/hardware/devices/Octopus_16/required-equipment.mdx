# What equipment is needed except Octopus 16  

- Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3-2.4GHz Wi-Fi, BLE 5.0, Dual Core    
![Image](/images/hardware/2026/01/octopos_3.png)  

When you buy it, the pins are usually not soldered; pay attention!   

- Power Bank (not more than 300 mAh and 5V, current not more than 2A)
