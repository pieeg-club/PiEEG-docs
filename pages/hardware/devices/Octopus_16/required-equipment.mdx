# What's in the Box

## Not included

| Item | Requirements | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3** | The controller that sits on top and sends data over Bluetooth. Comes with a small **BLE antenna**. Usually sold **without soldered pins**, so solder the headers first or buy a pre-soldered version. | [How to Start](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/setup) |
| **5 V power bank** | Small and light, 5 V output, **no more than 2 A**. Powers the board while it's worn. | [Safety](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/safety) |
| **USB-C data cable** | For flashing the firmware once, and for the power bank. Must be a **data** cable for flashing. | [Firmware](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/firmware) |
| **Strap or headband** | Holds the sensor on the head. An elastic band works, or a [3D-printed holder](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7386761). | [Pins & Placement](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/connections) |
| **A device with Bluetooth LE** | A computer or Android device with **Chrome or Edge** (for PiEEG Cloud and octoBridge), or Python 3.10+ for the Python SDK | [Software](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/software) |

![Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3](/images/hardware/2026/10/octopus16_xiao_s3.jpg)

<Callout type="warning">
  **Charge-only cables can't flash.** If no serial port appears when you plug in the XIAO, try a different USB-C cable before anything else.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Safari, Firefox and iPhone / iPad browsers don't support Web Bluetooth. Use Chrome or Edge.
</Callout>

## Checklist

- [ ] Octopus 16
- [ ] XIAO ESP32-S3 with headers soldered, and its BLE antenna
- [ ] USB-C data cable
- [ ] Charged small 5 V power bank (≤ 2 A)
- [ ] Strap or headband
- [ ] Chrome or Edge on a device with Bluetooth LE
