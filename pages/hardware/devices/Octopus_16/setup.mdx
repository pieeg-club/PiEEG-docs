# How to Start

From the box to 16 live channels in your browser, in seven steps.

<Steps>

### Gather the parts

You need the Octopus 16, a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 with its antenna, a USB-C data cable, a small 5 V power bank and a strap. See [What's in the Box](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/required-equipment).

### Mount the XIAO the right way round

1. Solder the headers onto the XIAO ESP32-S3 if it came without pins.
2. Line up the XIAO with the sockets on the Octopus 16 so the **`5V`, `GND` and `3V3`** labels on both boards match.
3. Press it down gently until it's fully seated.
4. Clip the **BLE antenna** onto the XIAO's antenna connector.

![XIAO ESP32-S3 mounted on Octopus 16, with the antenna](/images/hardware/2026/10/octopus16_xiao_mounted.jpg)

<Callout type="warning">
  A XIAO mounted **180° the wrong way** still flashes and shows up on Bluetooth, but sends **no EEG data** (the LED fast double-blinks). Check the `5V / GND / 3V3` labels line up.
</Callout>

### Flash the firmware (first time only)

1. Open **[octobridge.pieeg.com/flash](https://octobridge.pieeg.com/flash)** in desktop **Chrome or Edge**.
2. Plug the XIAO into the computer with the USB-C **data** cable. The board must **not** be on anyone's head.
3. Click **Install Octopus-16**, choose the port and wait about 30 seconds.
4. Unplug and replug once. It now shows up over Bluetooth as **`Octopus-16-XXXX`**.

No port? Hold **BOOT**, tap **RESET**, release BOOT and install again. Full details: [Firmware](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/firmware).

### Power from the power bank

Unplug the computer's USB cable and connect the **power bank** to the XIAO's USB-C port. The LED blinks slowly while it waits for a connection.

<Callout type="error">
  The board has **no isolation** from its power supply. While it's on a head, it runs from the **power bank only**, never plugged into a computer or a wall charger.
</Callout>

When connected, the LED stays on and data streams at 250 samples per second.

### Put it on

1. Place the sensor over **Pz** (top-back of the head) for the alpha test, or over the **back of the head** (visual cortex).
2. Part the hair under the cluster so the pins reach the scalp.
3. Press it **straight down** and hold it with the strap. The power bank can sit under the strap at the back of the head.

### Check the signal

Sit still, relax the face and run these quick tests. If they behave as described, you are recording real biosignals.

| Test | What you should see |
|---|---|
| **EEG ok** (PiEEG Cloud / octoBridge) | Green when the signal size (RMS) is between 2 and 200 µV |
| **Close your eyes for 10 seconds** (sensor over Pz) | Alpha (8–13 Hz) rises, then drops when the eyes open |
| **Blink** (sensor on the forehead) | Large slow spikes on every channel |
| **Clench the jaw** | RMS and gamma jump, then settle. That is muscle, not brain activity. |
