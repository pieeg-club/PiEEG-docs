# What's in the Box

## Included

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **1 × JNEEG shield** | 8-channel ADS1299 board with low-noise voltage regulators, a status LED, a 40-pin header for the Jetson Nano and a 2.54 mm electrode header |

![JNEEG shield: ADS1299, low-noise regulators, status LED and 40-pin header](/images/hardware/2026/10/jneeg_pinout.png)

## Not included

| Item | Requirements | More |
|---|---|---|
| **NVIDIA Jetson Nano Developer Kit** | With a microSD card (32 GB or more) and NVIDIA's JetPack image | [How to Start](/hardware/devices/jneeg/setup) |
| **5 V power bank** | 5 V, at least **3 A**, capacity up to 10,000 mAh. Powers the Jetson, the shield and the monitor. | [Power bank](/hardware/accessories/power-bank) |
| **Electrodes** | 8 EEG electrodes (dry or wet Ag/AgCl) with 2.54 mm connectors, plus **2 ear-clip electrodes** for reference and bias | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |
| **EEG cap** | Holds the electrodes on the scalp (10-20 system) | [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap) |
| **Monitor** | Portable monitor powered from the power bank or the Jetson, never from a wall socket | [Safety](/hardware/devices/jneeg/safety) |
| **Keyboard and mouse** | Wired USB. Wireless devices add radio noise. | [Noise elimination](/hardware/signal-quality/noise-elimination) |
| **Conductive gel** | Only for wet electrodes | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |

![JNEEG kit: 1 JNEEG on the Jetson Nano, 2 ear-clip electrodes, 3 EEG cap, 4 power bank](/images/hardware/2026/10/jneeg_kit.jpg)

<Callout type="info">
  **Headless option:** skip the monitor, keyboard and mouse and open the PiEEG Server dashboard from a laptop on the same network. The laptop must run **on battery** and must never be cabled to the Jetson or the electrodes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Battery only.** Every device cabled to the JNEEG (the Jetson, the monitor, the keyboard) must run from the power bank. Never use the Jetson's wall power supply while electrodes are on a person.
</Callout>

## Checklist

- [ ] JNEEG shield
- [ ] Jetson Nano with JetPack on a microSD card
- [ ] Charged 5 V / 3 A power bank and a short, thick USB cable
- [ ] 8 electrodes and 2 ear clips
- [ ] EEG cap (and gel if you use wet electrodes)
- [ ] Battery-powered monitor, wired keyboard and mouse (or a battery-powered laptop for headless use)
