# Electrodes & Pinout

## Electrode connections

JNEEG has three kinds of electrode inputs.

| Input | Count | Electrode | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Channels 1–8** | 8 | Dry or wet Ag/AgCl, in the cap | Each channel measures the voltage between its electrode and the reference |
| **Reference** | 1 | Ear clip on one earlobe | The common 0 µV point that all 8 channels are measured against |
| **Bias** | 1 | Ear clip on the other earlobe | Actively drives the body to cancel common-mode noise such as 50/60 Hz mains hum |

![JNEEG: electrodes, reference and bias on the 2.54 mm header; ADS1299, regulators, status LED and 40-pin header](/images/hardware/2026/10/jneeg_pinout.png)

The electrode header is a standard **2.54 mm** pin header (marked **1-8 Elec**), so most EEG cables with 2.54 mm female connectors fit. See [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap).

<Callout type="info">
  The **reference and bias clips matter most**. A loose ear clip makes every channel noisy at once. Clean the earlobes and make sure both clips sit firmly.
</Callout>

## Suggested montage

Place the electrodes on the 10-20 system. This layout is the same as the PiEEG-8 default, so recordings can be compared:

| Channel | Position | Area | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fp1** | Front left | Blinks, eye movement |
| 2 | **Fp2** | Front right | Blinks, eye movement |
| 3 | **C3** | Centre left | Motor activity (right hand) |
| 4 | **C4** | Centre right | Motor activity (left hand) |
| 5 | **P7** | Back left, side | Parietal and temporal activity |
| 6 | **P8** | Back right, side | Parietal and temporal activity |
| 7 | **O1** | Back left | Alpha rhythm, vision |
| 8 | **O2** | Back right | Alpha rhythm, vision |

![International 10-20 electrode positions](/images/hardware/2024/08/New-Bitmap-Image-264x300.jpg)

You can use any other positions. Keep a note of which channel went where.

## Jetson Nano pinout

The shield talks to the Jetson Nano over **SPI** (`spi1` in `jetson-io`, which appears as `/dev/spidev0.0`). It uses these pins on the 40-pin header:

| Header pin | Signal | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **19** | SPI MOSI | Jetson → ADS1299 |
| **21** | SPI MISO | ADS1299 → Jetson |
| **23** | SPI SCK | Jetson → ADS1299 |
| **24** | SPI CS0 (chip select) | Jetson → ADS1299 |
| **7** | DRDY (data ready) | ADS1299 → Jetson |

The shield also takes its power and ground from the header.

<Callout type="info">
  **DRDY** goes low each time a new sample is ready (250 times per second). The Python scripts read it as **board pin 7** with `Jetson.GPIO`. PiEEG Server reads it as line **216** on `/dev/gpiochip0`.
</Callout>
