# What's in the Box

## Included

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **1 × Octopus 16 EEG board** | 16 dry pogo-pin electrodes, 2 × ADS131M08, with Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 and BLE antenna |
| **1 × All-in-One Starter Kit for Arduino** | Elecrow SEA0004T, Arduino Uno compatible, sensors and outputs mounted in one case |
| **Software** | Access to the OctoBio web app ([octobridge.pieeg.com](https://octobridge.pieeg.com/)) and firmware |

SKU **PIA24028P** · 570 g · Sold by PiEEG on Elecrow.

![Octopus 16 EEG board, top and underside](/images/hardware/2026/10/octobio_octopus16_pins.jpg)

## Not included

| Item | Why you need it |
|---|---|
| **5 V power bank** | Powers the Octopus 16 while it is worn. Keep it small; output current no more than 2 A. See [Octopus 16 equipment](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16/required-equipment). |
| **Computer with Bluetooth** | Runs **desktop Chrome or Edge** (Web Bluetooth + Web Serial). |
| **USB data cables** | One for the XIAO (USB-C), one for the starter kit. Needed for flashing and for the kit's serial link. |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Charge-only cables can't flash or stream.** If no serial port appears, try a different cable before anything else.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Safari, Firefox and phones are not supported. They don't offer Web Serial (and most don't offer Web Bluetooth).
</Callout>

## Checklist

- [ ] Octopus 16 with XIAO ESP32-S3 mounted and BLE antenna connected
- [ ] Starter kit in its case
- [ ] USB data cables
- [ ] Charged 5 V power bank
- [ ] Computer with Bluetooth, desktop Chrome or Edge
