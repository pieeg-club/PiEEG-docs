# Safety

**Read this before anyone puts it on.**

<Callout type="warning">
  The OctoBio Kit is **not a medical device** and hasn't been certified by any regulator for use with the human body. It's a learning and experimentation tool.
</Callout>

## Rules

1. **Battery only while worn.** Wear the Octopus 16 on battery power only, never while it's plugged into a computer or a wall charger. The board has no isolation from its power supply.
2. **Never connect the relay to mains voltage.**
3. **No diagnosis.** Focus and relax are uncalibrated learning indicators. Never use them for diagnosis, or as a measure of mood or ability.
4. **Implanted devices.** Don't use it with a pacemaker or other implanted device without asking a physician.
5. **Skin.** Stop if the skin becomes irritated.
6. **Pin care.** Press the pogo pins straight down and keep them clean with isopropyl alcohol. Don't use oil or grease on them.

## Power while worn

| Power source | While worn | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **5 V power bank** | ✅ Safe | The worn board is on its own battery, with no path to the mains. |
| **Laptop USB** | ❌ Not while worn | USB from a computer is only for flashing, with the board off the head. A plugged-in laptop links the electrodes to the mains. |
| **Wall charger** | ❌ Never | A wall charger connects the person to the mains through the electrodes. |

## In the classroom

- An adult checks the power source before each session.
- Flash and charge boards **before** the lesson, with the boards off the head.
- Clean the pins between students.
