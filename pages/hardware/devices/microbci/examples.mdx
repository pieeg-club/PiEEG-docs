# Signals & Datasets

What real MicroBCI recordings look like, and open data to practise with. All recordings use **dry electrodes, no gel**, at 250 samples per second.

## Example signals

### EEG: alpha test

Electrode at **Fz**, eyes closed and open in turn. Filtered to 8–12 Hz, so the alpha rhythm shows as bigger waves in the "closed" periods.

![Alpha test with MicroBCI: 8–12 Hz, eyes closed and open](/images/hardware/2026/10/microbci_alpha.png)

### EMG: chewing

Electrode at **Fz**. Chewing 4, 3, 2 and 1 times. Filtered to 0.5–40 Hz.

![Chewing artifacts (EMG) recorded with MicroBCI](/images/hardware/2026/10/microbci_emg.png)

### EOG: blinking

Electrodes on the forehead. Blinking 4, 3, 2 and 1 times. Filtered to 0.5–40 Hz.

![Blinking (EOG) recorded with MicroBCI](/images/hardware/2026/10/microbci_eog.png)

<Callout type="info">
  Chewing and blinking are muscle and eye signals, not brain activity. Learn to recognise them before you analyse EEG.
</Callout>

## Datasets

In the [`Dataset`](https://github.com/pieeg-club/MicroBCI/tree/main/Dataset) folder of the MicroBCI repository. CSV files with **8 columns (channels 1–8) and no header row**, values in µV.

| File | Content | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `alpha_2ch.csv` | Alpha test: eyes closed and open | 22,985 samples (about 92 s) |
| `EMG_Ch_8.csv` | Chewing 4-3-2-1 times | 7,065 samples (about 28 s) |
| `EOG_Ch_8.csv` | Blinking 4-3-2-1 times | 9,584 samples (about 38 s) |

The plots above each show one filtered channel. Filter the files and look through the channels to find the one with the clearest signal.

**Plot them** with [`1.Band_pass.py`](https://github.com/pieeg-club/MicroBCI/tree/main/SDK/For_dataset) or [`2.Band_pass_and_Wavelet.py`](https://github.com/pieeg-club/MicroBCI/tree/main/SDK/For_dataset). Because there is no header row, load them with `pd.read_csv(file, header=None)`.

<Callout type="info">
  Raw values carry large DC offsets. Always apply a band-pass filter (for example 1–40 Hz, or 8–12 Hz for alpha) before looking at the signal.
</Callout>
