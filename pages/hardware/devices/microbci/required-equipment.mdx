# What's in the Box

## Included

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **1 × MicroBCI shield** | 8-channel ADS1299 board with Nucleo headers and a 2.54 mm electrode header |

![MicroBCI shield](/images/hardware/2026/10/microbci_hand.jpg)

## Not included

| Item | Requirements | More |
|---|---|---|
| **NUCLEO-WB55 board** | STMicroelectronics NUCLEO-WB55RG (STM32WB55, Bluetooth LE). Its built-in ST-LINK is used to flash the firmware. | [Firmware](/hardware/devices/microbci/firmware) |
| **Micro-USB cable** | A **data** cable: for flashing from a computer, then for power from the power bank | [Firmware](/hardware/devices/microbci/firmware) |
| **5 V power bank** | Small: 5 V, up to 1 A, up to 1,000 mAh | [Power bank](/hardware/accessories/power-bank) |
| **Electrodes** | 8 EEG electrodes (dry or wet Ag/AgCl) with 2.54 mm connectors, plus **2 ear-clip electrodes** for reference and bias. Or an EMG / ECG kit. | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |
| **EEG cap** | Holds the electrodes on the scalp (10-20 system) | [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap) |
| **Computer** | Windows, macOS or Linux, with **STM32CubeProgrammer** (to flash once) and Python 3 with Bluetooth LE, or Chrome / Edge | [Software](/hardware/devices/microbci/software) |
| **Android phone (optional)** | For the MicroBCI Android app | [Software](/hardware/devices/microbci/software#android-app) |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Battery only.** Flash the board from the computer with **no electrodes on anyone**. Then unplug it and power it from the power bank for recording.
</Callout>

## Checklist

- [ ] MicroBCI shield
- [ ] NUCLEO-WB55 board
- [ ] Micro-USB data cable
- [ ] Small 5 V power bank, charged
- [ ] 8 electrodes and 2 ear clips
- [ ] EEG cap (and gel if you use wet electrodes)
- [ ] Computer with STM32CubeProgrammer and Python (or Chrome / Edge)
