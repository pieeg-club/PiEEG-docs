# How to Start

From the box to live EEG over Bluetooth, in seven steps.

<Steps>

### Gather the parts

You need the MicroBCI, a NUCLEO-WB55 board, a Micro-USB data cable, a small 5 V power bank, 8 electrodes with two ear clips, and a cap. See [What's in the Box](/hardware/devices/microbci/required-equipment).

### Flash the Nucleo (first time only)

The NUCLEO-WB55 needs the Bluetooth stack and the MicroBCI firmware once. You do this from a computer with **STM32CubeProgrammer**, over the Nucleo's built-in ST-LINK USB port:

1. Upload the Bluetooth stack `stm32wb5x_BLE_Stack_full_fw.bin` at address **`0x080CE000`**.
2. Upload the firmware `Micro_BCI.hex`.

Step by step, with screenshots: [Firmware](/hardware/devices/microbci/firmware). Video: [youtu.be/crqOmnJ3Hjg](https://youtu.be/crqOmnJ3Hjg).

### Mount the shield

1. Unplug the Nucleo.
2. Line up the MicroBCI with the Nucleo's headers, matching the end pin marked by the arrow in the photo below.
3. Press it down evenly until all pins are seated.

![MicroBCI on the Nucleo: match the end pin marked by the arrow](/images/hardware/2026/10/microbci_alignment.jpg)

<Callout type="warning">
  **Pay attention to the alignment.** A shield shifted by one pin won't work. Compare with the photo before powering on.
</Callout>

### Connect the electrodes

| Connector | Electrode | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Channels 1–8** | 8 EEG electrodes in the cap | Scalp positions of the 10-20 system |
| **Reference** | Ear-clip electrode | One earlobe |
| **Bias** | Ear-clip electrode | The other earlobe |

![Wiring of channels 1–8, reference and bias](/images/hardware/2026/10/microbci_connection.png)

Details: [Electrodes & Mounting](/hardware/devices/microbci/connections).

### Power on from the battery

Plug the **power bank** into the Nucleo's Micro-USB port. The board starts advertising over Bluetooth.

<Callout type="error">
  MicroBCI has **no isolation**. While electrodes are on a person, the Nucleo runs from the **power bank only**, never from a computer's USB port or a wall charger.
</Callout>

### Connect over Bluetooth

You don't need to pair it in your system's Bluetooth settings. It uses the same Bluetooth protocol as IronBCI.

### Check the signal

Sit still, relax the face and run these quick tests.

| Test | What you should see |
|---|---|
| **Sit still for 30 seconds** | All 8 channels settle. Flat lines or huge values mean a loose electrode. |
| **Close your eyes for 10 seconds** | Alpha (8–12 Hz) rises, then drops when the eyes open |
| **Blink** (electrodes on the forehead) | Large slow spikes |
| **Chew or clench the jaw** | Bursts of high-frequency noise. That is muscle (EMG), not brain activity. |

![Alpha with eyes closed and open, recorded with MicroBCI](/images/hardware/2026/10/microbci_alpha.png)
