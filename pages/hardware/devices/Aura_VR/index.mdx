# Aura VR

**Aura VR** is an open-source hardware shield and wearable bio-signal interface designed by PiEEG. It captures real-time biometric and motion data streams—including EMG, EEG, and ECG—and sends them directly into VR, AR, and PC applications.

---

## Key Features

- **Motion Tracking & Movement Control:** Features an onboard **LSM6DS3TR-C** 6-axis IMU with an integrated 3D gyroscope and accelerometer, enabling direct motion and head-tracking controls inside virtual environments.
- **Muscle-Based Interactions:** Captures real-time EMG muscle signals, allowing you to trigger extra skills, gestures, or action inputs in VR without pressing physical buttons, as well as track athletic effort in sports performance applications.
- **Orientation Precision:** Select hardware revisions include an **LIS3MDL** 3-axis magnetometer for enhanced rotational orientation accuracy. *(Note: Check your specific hardware version for magnetometer inclusion.)*
- **Electrode Compatibility:** Connects to both dry and Ag/AgCl wet electrodes via standard 2.54 mm pin-pitch headers.
