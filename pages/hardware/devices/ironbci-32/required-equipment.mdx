# What's in the Box

## Included

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **1 × IronBCI-32** | 70 × 70 mm board with 4 × AD7771, STM32H743, two Micro USB ports (power and data) and four 2.54 mm electrode headers |

![IronBCI-32 board, top view](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci32_top.jpg)

## Not included

| Item | Requirements | More |
|---|---|---|
| **5 V USB battery** | A USB power bank. Powers the board through its **Power** port. | [Power bank](/hardware/accessories/power-bank) |
| **2 × Micro USB cables** | One for power, one for data. The data cable must be a **full data cable**, not charge-only. | [How to Start](/hardware/devices/ironbci-32/setup) |
| **Laptop or desktop** | Windows, macOS or Linux, with Python 3.10+ or Chrome / Edge. **Must run on battery** while recording. | [Safety](/hardware/devices/ironbci-32/safety) |
| **Electrodes** | Up to 32 EEG electrodes (dry or wet Ag/AgCl) with 2.54 mm connectors, plus **2 ear-clip electrodes** for reference and bias | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |
| **EEG cap** | A 32-channel cap. The [PiEEG 32-channel wet cap](https://www.pieeg.com/hardware/32ch-wet-cap) matches the layout in [Electrodes & Pinout](/hardware/devices/ironbci-32/connections#electrode-layout). | [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap) |
| **Conductive gel** | For wet electrodes | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |
| **Box (optional)** | 3D-printable enclosure | [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/PiEEG/designs) |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Charge-only cables can't carry data.** A charge-only cable has 2 wires; a data cable has 4. If the laptop doesn't see a new serial port, try a different cable before anything else.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Battery only.** The board runs from the USB battery, and the laptop connected to the data port must run on its own battery, unplugged from the wall charger.
</Callout>

## Checklist

- [ ] IronBCI-32
- [ ] Charged 5 V USB battery
- [ ] 2 Micro USB cables (at least one full data cable)
- [ ] Laptop on battery, with BrainFlow, PiEEG Server or Chrome / Edge
- [ ] 32 electrodes and 2 ear clips
- [ ] 32-channel cap and conductive gel
