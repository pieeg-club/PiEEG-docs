# IronBCI-32

**32 channels of EEG over a single USB cable.**

IronBCI-32 is a low-cost, open-source 32-channel, 24-bit biosignal acquisition board. Four 8-channel **AD7771** converters measure EEG, EMG, ECG and EOG across the whole head, and an **STM32H7** microcontroller streams all 32 channels to your laptop over USB. It works out of the box with **BrainFlow**, **PiEEG Server** and **PiEEG Cloud**.

![IronBCI-32 board](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci32_board.jpg)

<Callout type="warning">
  IronBCI-32 is **not a medical device**. Power it **from a 5 V battery only**, and run the laptop on battery too, unplugged from the wall. Read [Safety](/hardware/devices/ironbci-32/safety) before the first recording.
</Callout>

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Channels** | 32 biosignal inputs, plus reference and bias |
| **Converters** | 4 × AD7771, 8 channels each, 24-bit |
| **Microcontroller** | STM32H743 (Arm Cortex-M7) |
| **Sample rate** | About 500 SPS (BrainFlow reports 512 Hz) |
| **Signals** | EEG, EMG, ECG, EOG |
| **Link to the computer** | USB serial (virtual COM port), 921,600 baud |
| **Electrodes** | Dry or wet Ag/AgCl, 2.54 mm headers |
| **Power** | 5 V USB battery only, on its own USB port |
| **Board** | 70 × 70 mm, 4 layers |
| **Software** | BrainFlow (`IRONBCI_32_BOARD`), PiEEG Server, PiEEG Cloud |

## How it works

Two USB ports: one for power from a battery, one for data to a laptop.

```
 32 electrodes ──► 4 × AD7771 ──► STM32H7 ──USB──► Laptop (on battery)
 Ref + bias (ears)  (8 ch each)      ▲                ├ BrainFlow
                                     │                ├ PiEEG Server
           5 V USB battery ──USB─────┘                └ cloud.pieeg.com
```

1. **Measure:** four AD7771 chips digitise 8 channels each, all 32 at the same moment.
2. **Send:** the STM32H7 packs every sample into one frame and streams it over USB as a serial port. Streaming starts as soon as the board is powered.
3. **Use:** read it in BrainFlow, PiEEG Server or the browser, in any language.

![Power and data USB ports on IronBCI-32](/images/hardware/2026/10/ironbci32_power_data.jpg)

## Which board?

| | PiEEG-16 | IronBCI-8 | IronBCI-32 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Channels** | 16 | 8 | **32** |
| **Host** | Raspberry Pi | None (Bluetooth) | Laptop or desktop (USB) |
| **Converters** | 2 × ADS1299 | 1 × ADS1299 | 4 × AD7771 |
| **Sample rate** | 250 SPS | 250 SPS | **~500 SPS** |
| **BrainFlow** | No | No | **Yes** |
| **Best for** | Pi-based research | Wearable, mobile | Full-head, high-density EEG |

## What you can measure

| Signal | What it shows | Typical first test |
|---|---|---|
| **EEG** | Brain rhythms across the whole scalp | Close your eyes and watch alpha (8–13 Hz) rise at the back of the head |
| **EMG** | Muscle activity | Clench the jaw or a fist |
| **ECG** | Heart activity | Electrodes on the chest or wrists |
| **EOG** | Eye movement and blinks | Look left and right, blink |
