# What's in the Box

## Included

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **1 × ardEEG shield** | 8-channel ADS1299 board with Arduino UNO headers and a 2.54 mm electrode header |

![ardEEG shield](/images/hardware/2026/10/ardeeg_shield.jpg)

## Not included

| Item | Requirements | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Arduino UNO R4 WiFi** | The **WiFi** version is needed: the data goes over Wi-Fi | [How to Start](/hardware/devices/ardeeg/setup) |
| **5 V power bank** | Small: 5 V, **up to 1 A**, capacity **up to 1,000 mAh** | [Power bank](/hardware/accessories/power-bank) |
| **USB-C cable** | To upload the sketch once, then to connect the power bank | [Software](/hardware/devices/ardeeg/software) |
| **Electrodes** | 8 EEG electrodes (dry or wet Ag/AgCl) with 2.54 mm connectors, plus **2 ear-clip electrodes** for reference and bias | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |
| **EEG cap** | Holds the electrodes on the scalp (10-20 system) | [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap) |
| **Computer** | With Python 3, on the **same Wi-Fi network** as the Arduino. Runs the Arduino IDE once. | [Software](/hardware/devices/ardeeg/software) |
| **Wi-Fi network** | 2.4 GHz network the Arduino can join | [Software](/hardware/devices/ardeeg/software) |
| **Conductive gel** | Only for wet electrodes | [Electrodes](/hardware/accessories/electrodes) |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Battery only.** After uploading the sketch, unplug the computer and power the Arduino from the power bank. Never record while the Arduino is plugged into a computer or a wall charger.
</Callout>

## Checklist

- [ ] ardEEG shield
- [ ] Arduino UNO R4 WiFi
- [ ] Small 5 V power bank (≤ 1 A, ≤ 1,000 mAh), charged
- [ ] USB-C cable
- [ ] 8 electrodes and 2 ear clips
- [ ] EEG cap (and gel if you use wet electrodes)
- [ ] Computer with Python and the Arduino IDE, on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network
