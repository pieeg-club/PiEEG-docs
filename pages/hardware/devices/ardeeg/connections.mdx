# Electrodes & Pinout

## Electrode connections

ardEEG has three kinds of electrode inputs.

| Input | Count | Electrode | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Channels 1–8** | 8 | Dry or wet Ag/AgCl, in the cap | Each channel measures the voltage between its electrode and the reference |
| **Reference** | 1 | Ear clip on one earlobe | The common 0 µV point that all 8 channels are measured against |
| **Bias** | 1 | Ear clip on the other earlobe | Actively drives the body to cancel common-mode noise such as 50/60 Hz mains hum |

![ardEEG wiring: channels 1–8, reference and bias](/images/hardware/2026/10/ardeeg_connection.png)

The electrode header is a standard **2.54 mm** pin header, so most EEG cables with 2.54 mm (0.1") female connectors fit. See [Cap](/hardware/accessories/cap).

<Callout type="info">
  The **reference and bias clips matter most**. A loose ear clip makes every channel noisy at once. Clean the earlobes and make sure both clips sit firmly.
</Callout>

## Suggested montage

These 8 positions spread across the front, centre, sides and back of the head:

| Position | Area | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **F7**, **F8** | Front left and right, side | Eye movement, frontal activity |
| **Fz** | Front centre | Blinks, attention; used for the example recordings |
| **C3**, **C4** | Centre left and right | Motor activity (hands) |
| **T5**, **T6** | Back left and right, side | Temporal activity |
| **Pz** | Back centre, top | Alpha rhythm |

![8-electrode arrangement on the 10-20 system, and the assembled kit](/images/hardware/2026/10/ardeeg_montage.jpg)

Assign them to channels 1–8 in any order, and keep a note of which channel went where.

## Arduino pinout

The shield uses only these Arduino pins, plus power and ground:

| Arduino pin | Signal |
|---|---|
| **D13** | SPI clock (SCK) |
| **D12** | SPI MISO (data from the ADS1299) |
| **D11** | SPI MOSI (data to the ADS1299) |
| **D10** | Chip select |
| **D7** | DRDY: *data ready* from the ADS1299 |

![ardEEG pins on the Arduino headers](/images/hardware/2026/10/ardeeg_pinout.png)

All other pins are free for your own sensors, buttons or outputs.

<Callout type="info">
  **DRDY** goes low each time a new sample is ready (250 times per second). The sketch waits for that edge, then reads 27 bytes over SPI. Details in [Specification](/hardware/devices/ardeeg/specification#data-format).
</Callout>
