# octoWatch — Garmin Connect IQ App for Octopus-16

**[octoWatch on Garmin Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/8e994535-c853-45ca-abe3-ccef61119c8b)** is the official Garmin smartwatch application for **[PiEEG Octopus-16](/hardware/devices/Octopus_16)**. It connects directly from your wrist to the Octopus-16 (Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 + dual Texas Instruments `ADS131M08` 24-bit ADCs) over Bluetooth Low Energy (`Toybox.BluetoothLowEnergy`) — streaming, filtering, and visualizing live 16-channel 250 SPS EEG with zero phone or laptop required in the middle.

## Key Features

- **Direct Wrist-to-Headset BLE GATT Link**
  - Automatically scans for, pairs with, and subscribes to Octopus-16 (`4fafc201-1fb5-459e-8fcc-c5c9c331914b`) without needing a smartphone or PC bridge.
  - Built-in connection watchdog and automatic reconnection if the link drops.
  - **250 SPS Built-In Simulator Mode**: Automatically starts after 6 seconds when no physical Octopus-16 is nearby so you can explore all 5 screens, synthetic 10 Hz Alpha / 22 Hz Beta / 5 Hz Theta waves, and frontal eye-blink artifacts (`Fp1`/`Fp2`) right on your watch.
- **Dual BLE Packet Framing (`0xA0` & `0xA1`)**
  - **Compact 20-Byte Batched Frame (`0xA1`)**: Default Garmin-optimized framing carrying 8 × 16-bit signed samples (`0.1 µV` resolution) per 20-byte GATT notification to fit standard Garmin BLE MTU limits at 250 SPS.
  - **Standard 51-Byte Frame (`0xA0 ... 0xC0`)**: Full 16-channel × 24-bit signed big-endian samples from ADC1 (`CH01–CH08`) and ADC2 (`CH09–CH16`).
- **On-Watch DSP & Signal Quality**
  - Zero-allocation 16-channel × 128-sample circular ring buffer (`~0.51 s` window at 250 SPS).
  - Online IIR high-pass DC blocker (`α = 0.992`) per channel to eliminate electrode drift and keep waveforms centered.
  - Live 64-sample RMS amplitude (`µV`), measured sampling rate (`SPS`), packet drop counter, relative spectral band estimators (`α` Calm, `β` Focus, `θ` Rest), and composite Signal Quality Index (`SQI%`).

## Quick Start: Connect Octopus-16 to Your Garmin Watch

<Steps>

### Install octoWatch on your Garmin watch

Open the **[Garmin Connect IQ Store page for octoWatch](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/8e994535-c853-45ca-abe3-ccef61119c8b)** in the **Garmin Connect IQ** mobile app or Garmin Express and click **Install**.

*(For developer USB sideloading, copy `octoWatch.prg` directly into your watch's `GARMIN/APPS/` folder over USB.)*

### Flash the Garmin-compatible firmware on Octopus-16

Flash the Octopus-16 ESP32-S3 with the Garmin-optimized BLE firmware (supports both 20-byte `0xA1` batched notifications and remote channel switching `[0xC0, ch]`), or install from **[firmware.pieeg.com](https://firmware.pieeg.com/)**.

### Put on the Octopus-16 headset (Fit Guide)

1. **Power & Wrist:** Wear your Garmin watch on your **left wrist** for the cleanest line-of-sight BLE path and power on the Octopus-16 (`5 V` USB power bank).
2. **Ear Reference & BIAS:** Clip **REF** to your left earlobe (`A1`) and **BIAS** to your right earlobe (`A2`).
3. **10–20 Scalp Placement:** Seat `Fp1`/`Fp2` ~2 cm above your eyebrows and `O1`/`O2` over the occipital inion at the back of your head, parting hair under the spring-loaded pogo pins.
4. **Verify Contact (`SQI >= 80%`):** Switch to **`FIT GUIDE`** (Page 3) or **`16CH GRID`** (Page 5) on your watch and confirm the electrode nodes glow **green**.

### Stream & Navigate on Your Wrist

Launch **octoWatch** from your watch's Activities/Apps list. Within seconds it pairs with `Octopus-16` and switches to **`LIVE 250Hz`**.

</Steps>

## BLE GATT Protocol Summary

| Characteristic | UUID | Direction | Payload |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Octopus Service** | `4fafc201-1fb5-459e-8fcc-c5c9c331914b` | Primary Service | Advertised as `Octopus-16` / `PiEEG` |
| **EEG Data (`NOTIFY`)** | `beb5483e-36e1-4688-b7f5-ea07361b26a8` | Octopus-16 → Watch | `0xA1` 20-byte compact batch (8 × `int16` in `0.1 µV` units) or `0xA0..0xC0` 51-byte 16-ch frame |
| **Command (`WRITE`)** | `c0de0001-36e1-4688-b7f5-ea07361b26a8` | Watch → Octopus-16 | `[0xC0, channelId]` selects active channel (`0..15`) for 20-byte batch streaming |
