# octoBridge

**[octobridge.pieeg.com](https://octobridge.pieeg.com/)** is the PiEEG classroom lab in the browser. Wear **Octopus-16**, plug in the **octoBio Kit**, watch live readings, and run programs on the board. No Arduino IDE, no PiEEG Server, no account.

Project name: **octobridge**. Product name in the app: **octoBridge**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Classroom tool. Not a medical device. Band power, focus, and relax are uncalibrated classroom indexes, not a diagnosis.
</Callout>

## Open it

<Steps>
### Use Chrome or Edge

Desktop Chrome or Edge on HTTPS. Safari and phones cannot open USB serial. Bluetooth and USB stay in this tab.

### Open the lab

Go to **[octobridge.pieeg.com](https://octobridge.pieeg.com/)**.

### Flash, then connect

First time, open [Flash](https://octobridge.pieeg.com/flash). Then [Connect](https://octobridge.pieeg.com/connect). Skip a device you do not have. Numbers stay still until that device is live.

</Steps>

## Two devices

| Device | Link | What it is |
|--------|------|------------|
| **Octopus-16** | Bluetooth | 16-channel biosignal sensor. The browser computes relative band power in the tab. Appears as `Octopus-16-XXXX` after flashing. |
| **octoBio Kit** | USB serial, 115200 baud | Arduino Uno (ATmega328P) board: room sensors, lights, relay, buzzer, servo, and a 16×2 screen. |

You can use one without the other.

## What you can read

**octoBio Kit:** light (lux), distance (cm; needs a facing surface), temperature (°C), humidity (%), moisture (ADC 0–1023), sound (ADC), knob (ADC), button, PIR motion, IR remote codes, and 6-axis tilt (no heading).

**Octopus-16:** relative delta, theta, alpha, beta, and gamma; uncalibrated focus and relax indexes; RMS (µV); strongest EEG band; and an “EEG ok” check (RMS between 2 and 200 µV). Blink and jaw raise RMS and gamma.

## What you can move

On the octoBio Kit: light D10 (on, off, flash), light D8 (on, off), relay, buzzer, servo (0–180°), and the 16×2 screen.

Blocks can mix a reading into a move. Examples already in the lab: eyes closed → light on, walk-by → buzzer, knob → servo, focus → servo (focus meter), distance → light, alpha and no motion → a line on the screen.

## Flash firmware

From [octobridge.pieeg.com/flash](https://octobridge.pieeg.com/flash). Chrome or Edge, USB data cable. No Arduino IDE.

| Target | Board | How |
|--------|-------|-----|
| **Octopus-16** | Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 | **Install Octopus-16**. If no port appears, hold **BOOT**, tap **RESET**, release BOOT, then install again. Unplug and replug. It shows up over Bluetooth as `Octopus-16-XXXX`. |
| **Puck** | CrowPanel 2.1 rotary HMI | **Install Puck**. The panel links to an Octopus-16 over Bluetooth. No Wi-Fi. The image is 16 MB. |
| **octoBio Kit** | Arduino Uno / ATmega328P (official 16U2 or CH340) | Close any other serial monitor. **Flash octoBio Kit**. About 20 seconds. The screen should say octoBio Kit Ready. Not Leonardo, Mega, ESP32, or a board with no bootloader. |

When the octoBio Kit port opens, the board restarts. Wait until status is live. The kit speaks a line protocol at 115200 baud and answers `INF protocol=1 ready`.

## Limits

- Needs Chrome or Edge on HTTPS (the hosted site) or localhost.
- USB serial and Bluetooth stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded to a PiEEG server.
- Distance needs a facing surface. A missed echo is a negative number, not “very far”.
- Sound, moisture, and the knob are raw counts (0–1023), not calibrated units.
- Focus and relax are uncalibrated indexes. Do not treat them as mood or a clinical score.
